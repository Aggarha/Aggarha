Findings
01The icons aren't from a library — that's the problem

33 inline SVGs across 20 files, each re-declared where it's used. Stroke widths in the tree right now: 1.7 · 1.75 · 1.8 · 2 · 2.1 · 2.2 · 3 · 3.2. Sizes: 9 · 11 · 12 · 13 · 14 · 16 · 18 · 20. So the "inconsistent stroke boldness" complaint lands here too — but swapping icon sets fixes nothing, because there is no set. NAV_ICONS in system.tsx is the only shared map and only the bottom nav reads it.

02Trust and transaction-mode are the same two colours

VerifiedSparkle: verified #ccff00, premium #ff8a1f. ListingModeBadge: Rent #ccff00, Swap #ff8a1f. Identical hues, ~90px apart on every listing card. A lime sparkle next to a lime Rent chip reads as one signal, not two. This is the single highest-value fix and it's two hex values.

03The trust mark is the smallest thing on the card

14px, inline at the end of a text-xs text-white/50 location line, after a truncate that can push it to the edge. For a product whose whole pitch is trust, the signature mark currently ranks below the price, the title and the mode chip.

04Four accent families are live at once

Brand lime/orange · condition mint #58f0c6 / amber #ffd27a / salmon #ff9a8a · gold #d4af37 (new on this branch — coherent within Collectibles, but sealed off from the rest of the system) · PlayStation blue. Worse, condition severity "minor" is #ccff00 — so lime now also means "slight damage" on the same page it means Rent, verified, and primary action.

05The bottom nav can't tell you where you are

All five items render text-white/65 with no active variant — Home and Browse look identical from either screen. And Messages is a permanently disabled tab holding 20% of the primary navigation. Give the slot to Saved until Messages exists.

06Don't add category icons

The brief lists category icons as an audit target, but page.tsx renders categories as square photo tiles with a gradient scrim. That's already the more premium answer — real objects beat glyphs for a rental marketplace. Leave them; spend the icon work on nav, actions and empty states.

07Still carried over from before

Nine card greys and five radii, unchanged. Four visible "Coming soon" surfaces (StickyBookingCard's two disabled buttons, the Messages tab, MapPanel, NearbyCard distance). The triple-gradient body background still sits under every screen.
